**Standard:** Fail-fast — the brief's own statement of it: [[/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-agent-armory/779c0f74-86b0-5e8f-b02d-811e78ed3f66/scratchpad/audit/BRIEF.md@FILE:L49-L53]] "Engineering practice is fail-fast: surface an error where it occurs rather than masking it." A new `T-3-5` case with the `max_page_count` reproduction belongs in the plan's §12 (the tests are batch 6 part b's units). The `mustExist` classes feed Step 6's `repo_not_bound` detail and Step 28.
**Verdict:** replace — keep the nesting, the `mustExist` split and `backupFile`; replace the swallowed `ROLLBACK TO`/`RELEASE` and `ROLLBACK` catches with transaction-state detection that fails the unit of work visibly, and correct the false comment.
**Would be wrong if:** `node:sqlite` never auto-rolls-back inside a savepoint on `SQLITE_FULL` (the reproduction shows it does), or a caller-visible `StoreTransactionLost` were already raised somewhere in the adapter (it is not: the only throws are `StoreBusy`, `StoreMissing`, `StoreUnreadable` and the rethrown original).

## Candidates awaiting owner decision

### C1. Design/review skills need an explicit conceptual-fidelity (collapse) check
**Raised:** 2026-07-17 (from observations #1, #2, #4)
**Applies to:** all skills that produce or evaluate design/engineering decisions (expert-architecture-*, expert-standard, expert-review, expert-plan)
**Requirement:** Every such skill must include a check, distinct from citation
and structure verification, that tests each load-bearing decision against the
goal/mission it serves — "state the job in goal terms; write the hardest question
that exposes it as filler; answer it with a source citation or cut it." Citation
and structure gates do not catch a well-formed decision that fails to serve the
goal; only a purpose-fidelity check does.
**Propagation:** opportunistic (apply next time each skill is regenerated)
**Status:** candidate — not yet approved by owner

### C2. Establish, don't assert — verification claims must come from an actual check
**Raised:** 2026-07-17 (from observation #9)
**Applies to:** all skills that produce or evaluate engineering work (expert-standard, expert-review, expert-architecture-*, expert-plan, expert-spec)
**Requirement:** Every claim of correctness, completeness, or verification — a
computed total, a coverage or "all/every" statement, a "verified" label, a
passed gate, a delivered control — must be produced by an actual check against
source at the time it is written, never from expectation of what should be true.
When reviewing, a cluster of findings that all reduce to "asserted but never
established" signals rebuild-over-patch, not spot-fixes.
**Propagation:** opportunistic
**Status:** candidate — not yet approved by owner
